Flight debrief

    How to fly

    New to rockets? Open 🎓 Learn for animated lessons, or 🧭 Guide me for a step-by-step first launch.

    1. Design. Pick a nose cone, body shape, fins, engines and tank sizes for up to three stages. The analysis panel shows mass, thrust-to-weight, Δv and stability.
    2. Launch. The flight computer follows the flight plan you chose. Spent stages drop off automatically.
    3. Reach orbit. You need roughly 3,400 m/s of Δv and a periapsis above the 70 km atmosphere.

    What makes a rocket fail

    • Thrust-to-weight below 1: it never leaves the pad.
    • Unstable aerodynamics (centre of pressure ahead of centre of mass) with weak steering: it tumbles and snaps.
    • Too much drag or too little Δv: it runs out of fuel and falls back.
    • Too much thrust low down, or a weak body: dynamic pressure tears it apart.
